## Changed

- The extracted download opens from **Simple Video Editor.exe** at the top of the folder. Runtime files are bundled into the executable, and video tools, guides, and licenses live in **Support**.
- Moving an existing timeline clip uses the clip block without a floating thumbnail. Library drags keep their thumbnail preview.

## Fixed

- Dragging a clip keeps you at the same frame before, during, and after the move. A simple click seeks when you release the mouse, so starting a drag does not jump to another frame.

## Known limitations

- This release supports Windows x64, SDR video, one linked video/audio track, and the first audio stream. HDR, independent audio editing, effects, titles, and transitions are outside its scope.
