## Changed

- The extracted download opens from **Simple Video Editor.exe** at the top of the folder. Runtime files are bundled into the executable, video tools live in **bin**, and guides and licenses live in **docs**.
- Moving an existing timeline clip uses the clip block without a floating thumbnail. Library drags keep their thumbnail preview.

## Fixed

- Moving a clip or dragging its edges keeps the playhead at the frame you were viewing. If an edge trim removes that frame, the preview moves to the nearest retained frame. A simple click seeks when you release the mouse.

## Known limitations

- This release supports Windows x64, SDR video, one linked video/audio track, and the first audio stream. HDR, independent audio editing, effects, titles, and transitions are outside its scope.
