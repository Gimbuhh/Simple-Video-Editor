## Breaking Changes

- Projects saved by 1.1 use a new format that older versions cannot read. Keep your original project files and the 1.0.4 app available for rollback.

## Added

- HDR footage can stay in HDR through preview and export, or convert to SDR when needed.
- Audio controls let you choose a track, change volume, mute or fade each section.
- Framing controls fit, fill, crop or rotate individual sections while leaving recordings untouched.
- HEVC (H.265) is available alongside H.264 and AV1 export.

## Changed

- The editor runs on Rust with a smaller portable package. The compared candidate's download is about 25% smaller than 1.0.4 and omits the bundled .NET runtime.
- Frame navigation and quick trims use the actual frames in variable-frame-rate recordings, including game captures.

## Known limitations

- This is an unreleased candidate. Files passed to the executable are ignored, timeline drops append to the end, and idle CPU use needs improvement. See the [C#/Rust comparison](../docs/rust-csharp-comparison.md).
- A later visual refinement pass aims to match or improve on the C# interface. Hard cuts remain the default; transition effects are follow-up work.
- Startup, display switching and accessibility acceptance remain under review. See the [rewrite status](../docs/rust-rewrite-status.md) for the remaining checks.
