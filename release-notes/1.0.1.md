## Fixed

- Freely dragging a clip keeps the white playhead steady at the clip's start, making placement easier to follow.
- Export cleanup gives temporary files a moment to unlock before retrying, including when you cancel an export.

## Known limitations

- This release supports Windows x64, SDR video, one linked video/audio track, and the first audio stream. HDR, independent audio editing, effects, titles, and transitions are outside its scope.
