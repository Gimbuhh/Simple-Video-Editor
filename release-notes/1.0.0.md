## Added

- A searchable recording library lets you preview recordings and drag them into a timeline without changing the original files.
- Video and audio stay linked as you move, trim, split, or duplicate clips. Nearby clip edges snap together, and audio waveforms help locate moments to edit.
- Quick trims keep everything before or after the current frame, alongside the normal split command and undo/redo.
- Playback follows the edited sequence and keeps playing or paused as you scrub. AV1 and H.264 export support CPU or NVIDIA encoding with quality presets and cancellation.
- Saved projects, session recovery, missing-file relinking, and cache cleanup help you return to your edits.
- The complete portable ZIP runs without an installer or separate runtime/video-tool setup.

## Known limitations

- This release supports Windows x64, SDR video, one linked video/audio track, and the first audio stream. HDR, independent audio editing, effects, titles, and transitions are outside its scope.
- Preview can briefly pause between recordings; these loading pauses are absent from exports. Shadowplay capture targeting still needs checking with a game running.
